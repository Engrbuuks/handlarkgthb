Free trial

Every new workspace starts with a free trial of 14 days, or 44 days when it was created through a customer's referral link. No card is needed. During the trial a workspace may have up to 15 people.

When the trial ends without a plan, nothing is deleted. The workspace becomes read only until the owner chooses a plan. The owner can still see and export everything, and each member can still see and export everything they can see: their tasks, their time and the meetings they attend.
