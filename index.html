Questions

Answers Before You Sign Up

Still wondering?

Write to us and a real person replies.

hello@example.comEmail us
Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Create an account and use everything for 14 days. Choose a plan in Settings when you are ready.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. The workspace becomes read only until you choose a plan, so you and your team can still see and export everything.

Is it cheaper to pay yearly?

Yes. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free. If a customer you refer starts paying, you also get a free month.

When my clients pay an invoice online, who holds the money?

You do. Clients pay into your own Paystack account using your key. The money never passes through us, and the invoice is marked paid as soon as Paystack confirms it.

Will my team feel watched?

No. The procrastination tools are there to help each person, not to grade them. You see which tasks might be stuck and who is in a focus session right now. Why someone felt stuck, how they felt and their personal patterns are private to them.

Can my clients see their tasks?

Yes. Each client gets a private link to their own page, with progress, comments, approvals and invoices, and no login. Private tasks never show there.

How does WhatsApp work?

The owner connects a WhatsApp Business number once. Each person turns alerts on for themselves, then gets new tasks, reminders and approvals on WhatsApp and can reply TASKS, DONE 2, UPDATE 2 or IN to work without opening the app.

What does the AI do, and does it see private work?

It writes the daily summary from the updates your team posted, and turns pasted messages or voice notes into tasks you check before adding. Private tasks are never sent to it, and nothing is added without your tap.

Can we hold meetings in Handlark?

Yes. Set up a meeting with a free video room, or paste your own Zoom, Google Meet or Teams link. Everyone gets an invitation in their own time zone, a reminder the day before and a nudge just before it starts. During the call you tick the agenda and write notes together, then each action item becomes a task and the follow up email goes to everyone. Clients can join from their page and ask for a meeting there too.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It runs in any browser and installs on the home screen of iPhone and Android phones like an app, with deadline alerts.

Will it work on a weak connection?

Yes. Changes are kept on the device and sent when the connection returns, and the app opens offline with the last saved view.

Where is my data kept?

In a secure database and private file storage. Each workspace is locked to its own members, files open through short lived links, and you can export all tasks as a spreadsheet at any time.

Can I pay in naira?

Yes. Paystack takes cards, bank transfer and USSD in naira. Teams abroad can pay in US dollars by card.
