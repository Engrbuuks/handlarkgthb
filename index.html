Who it is for

Staff

Kind by design

Accountability without spying

People do their best work when they are trusted and can see what is measured about them. Handlark is built from the work itself, never from watching people.

Your own data first

Each person sees their own patterns, review flags and attendance before anyone else. Leads see focus and meeting time only as one total for everyone they look after, when that is 3 or more people.

Wellbeing that stays private

A gentle still working check

Answers before you sign up

Still wondering?

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. Create an account and use everything for 14 days. Choose a plan in Settings when you are ready.

We do not bill clients. Is it still for us?

Yes. When you create a workspace, choose Company or growing team, or NGO or field team. Clients are then called departments or projects, and invoices, rates and billing stay out of the menus until you turn on We bill clients in Settings. You can change the kind of team later.

Does it work for staff in the office, in the field and at home?

Yes. Everyone uses the same app on a phone or computer. Clock in can check the location against your work sites, planned shifts follow each person's own time zone, and deadlines mean the same moment for everyone.

What happens when the trial ends?

Nothing is deleted. The workspace becomes read only until you choose a plan. The owner can still see and export everything, and each person on the team can still see and export everything they can see: their tasks, their time and the meetings they attend.

Is it cheaper to pay yearly?

Yes. A yearly plan costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free. If a customer you refer starts paying, you also get a free month.

What is the founding offer?

While it runs, a new subscriber gets the percent off shown above for as long as they keep their subscription. It ends on the date shown, or sooner when the founding spots are taken. Customers who subscribe later pay the normal price, and nobody's price changes because of it.

Will my team feel watched?

No. Nobody is watched by default: no screenshots, no keystroke logging, no webcam. You see which tasks might be stuck and who is in a focus session right now. Why someone felt stuck, how they felt, their own estimates and their personal patterns are private to them. If a client needs proof of hours, you can turn on optional Proof mode for that client only. Each person reads what it does and agrees first (you can never agree for them), screenshots are taken in their own browser only while they share their screen and their timer runs, they see every shot first and can delete any within 10 minutes, and they can pause or withdraw. Keystrokes, apps and websites are never tracked. The budget vs actual report compares time with the budget you set, never with anyone's own guess, and each person sees the flags and checks about their own time.

What do the insights show, and to whom?

Each person sees their own first, under My patterns: focus time, meetings, other time, utilization, their next two weeks and gentle wellbeing signals with a private suggestion. You, your managers and team leads see hours, billable time, utilization and capacity per person for the people you look after, and focus and meeting time only as one total for everyone they look after, when that is 3 or more people, rounded to the hour, never for one team picked out. Wellbeing reaches a lead only as a count shared by 2 or more people among everyone they look after, never names, unless someone chooses to share theirs. Nobody is ever ranked.

How does Proof mode work, and what does it cost?

It is off until you switch it on for a client. Then, while someone times work for that client on a computer and shares their screen in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, the app takes 1 to 3 screenshots at random in each 10 minutes, blurred unless you choose sharp. Each one waits 10 minutes in the person’s Proof tray, where they can delete it, before you see it. Time with no screenshots is flagged no proof, never stopped or deleted. Screenshots are kept 30 days by default (7, 14, 30 or 60, your choice) and count toward your file storage. No extra app, no extra price.

Can my clients see their work?

Yes, if you work for clients. Each client gets a private link to their own page, with progress, comments, approvals and invoices, and no login. Private tasks never show there.

When my clients pay an invoice online, who holds the money?

You do. Clients pay into your own Paystack account using your key. The money never passes through us, and the invoice is marked paid as soon as Paystack confirms it.

How does WhatsApp work?

It works out of the box through the shared Handlark number. An owner who wants messages to come from their own business can connect their own WhatsApp Business number once instead. Each person turns alerts on for themselves, then gets new tasks, reminders and approvals on WhatsApp and can reply TASKS, DONE 2, UPDATE 2 or IN to work without opening the app.

What does the AI do, and does it see private work?

It writes the daily summary from the updates your team posted, and turns pasted messages or voice notes into tasks you check before adding. Private tasks are never sent to it, and nothing is added without your tap. A voice note sent on WhatsApp comes back as a proposed task, added only when you reply YES.

Will it work on a weak connection?

Yes. Changes are kept on the device and sent when the connection returns, and the app opens offline with the last saved view. It installs on iPhone and Android phones like an app.

Where is my data kept?

In a secure database and private file storage. Each workspace is locked to its own members, files open through short lived links, and you can export everything as spreadsheets at any time. The privacy policy has the details.

Does it work in French?

Yes. Each person picks English or French, and their emails follow. Clients see their page in their browser's language.

Where do I get help?

The help centre has a guide for every feature, for owners, managers, team leads, members and clients. You can also email us, and chat with us when chat is on.

Can I pay in naira?

Yes. Paystack takes cards, bank transfer and USSD in naira. Teams abroad can pay in US dollars by card.
