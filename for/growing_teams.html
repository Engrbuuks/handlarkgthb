How Handlark helps

What solves it

Departments, not clients

Choose Company or growing team and the app talks about departments. Billing stays out of the menus until you need it.

Clock in at the right place

Clock in from any phone, with breaks and an optional location check against your work sites. Away from site is flagged, never blocked.

Shifts and attendance

Weekly or one off shifts with swaps. Late, missed and left early go to the person first; their lead hears only if nothing is explained.

Leave without the notebook

Leave types with balances that build up each month, carry over and come off on approval, plus public holidays.

Payroll from approved hours

Overtime rules, expenses and approved hours flow into payroll; pay in naira through your own Paystack, with payslips.

Managers and team leads

Leads approve timesheets, leave and claims for their own people. Money and settings stay yours unless you share them.

WhatsApp alerts and replies

New tasks and reminders on WhatsApp; staff reply DONE 2 or IN without opening the app.

Made for weak connections

Changes are saved on the phone and sent when the network is back. Nothing typed is lost.
