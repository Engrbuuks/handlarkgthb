How Handlark helps

What solves it

Projects, not clients

Choose NGO or field team and work is grouped by project. Billing stays out of the menus unless you turn it on.

Work sites and location check

Clock in checks the location against your work sites and flags away from site. It never stops anyone clocking in.

Field visits and reports as templates

A field visit or a monthly report becomes dated steps for each person, every time.

Photos and files on each task

Visit reports, photos and receipts stay with the task, opened through short lived private links.

Attendance per person and team

Shifts, on time, late, missed and leave, by person and by team, ready to export.

Works on a weak network

Changes are kept on the phone and sent when the connection comes back.

Updates by WhatsApp

Field staff get tasks on WhatsApp and reply DONE 2 with a note, or send a voice note that becomes a task.

Expenses with receipts

Receipts in, approvals out, and payroll shows what to repay.
