How we keep your work safe

Your workspace holds client work, staff records and invoices, so security is built into every part of Handlark. This page explains the main measures in plain words.

Sign in and two step sign in

  • Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. Handlark never sees or stores your password.
  • Every request carries a sign in token, and our server checks its signature against Google's public keys before doing anything.
  • Anyone can turn on two step sign in with an authenticator app. Owners can require it for everyone in the workspace.
  • After 5 wrong codes, codes pause for 15 minutes, longer if it keeps happening, and the person gets an email.
  • Joining a workspace needs a one time invite link or a confirmed email address, so nobody can join by typing someone else's email.

Encryption

  • All traffic to the website, the app and our server uses HTTPS, and browsers are told to always use it.
  • The database and file storage are encrypted at rest by our providers.
  • Secrets we hold for customers, such as an agency's own Paystack key and authenticator secrets, are encrypted again with our own key before they are stored, and Paystack keys are never sent back to a browser.

Who can see what

  • Row level security is switched on for every database table, and the public database roles have no access at all. Every read and write goes through our server functions, which check that the person belongs to the workspace.
  • Workspace rules are enforced on the server: private tasks, hidden people and owner only settings cannot be reached by changing the app.
  • Managers never see a person's private reasons, moods or patterns from the procrastination tools.

Private files

  • Files are kept in a private storage bucket. Each link to a file lasts one hour and is only given to people who can see the task.
  • Uploads are limited to pictures, PDFs, Office documents, text, CSV, zip files and voice notes, up to 10 MB each. The server checks what actually arrived before a file is attached, and files that could run code download instead of opening in the browser.

History and control

  • Owners can see a history of every change in the workspace, with what it was before and after. Secrets never appear in it.
  • Deleted tasks go to a bin for 30 days and can be restored.
  • Sign out everywhere, in each person's account, signs out every device at once, including the one in use.
  • Owners can export every task as a spreadsheet at any time.

Platform protections

  • Payment and WhatsApp notifications are accepted only with a valid signature, and each one is applied once.
  • Limits on how often actions can be tried stop people from guessing codes or flooding others with email.
  • The website sends strict security headers: it cannot be framed by other sites, it sends no referrer, and only our own scripts may run.

Reporting a security problem

If you believe you have found a security weakness in Handlark, please email hello@example.com with "Security" in the subject. Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it and how it could affect users.

  • We will confirm we received your report within 3 business days and keep you updated until it is fixed.
  • Give us a reasonable time, normally up to 90 days, to fix the problem before you share it publicly.
  • Only test with accounts and workspaces you own. Do not access, change or delete other people's data, do not run denial of service or spam tests, and stop and tell us as soon as you reach data that is not yours.
  • If you act in good faith and follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you, and we will thank you publicly if you wish.

We do not run a paid bug bounty at the moment.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your data, we will tell the affected workspace owners without undue delay, explain what happened and what we are doing about it, and notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission and other authorities where the law requires.
