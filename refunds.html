Refunds and cancellation

When Handlark refunds a payment, how to cancel and how to ask for your money back.

Last updated 14 October 2026

In short: a full refund within 7 days of your first paid charge, duplicated or mistaken charges always refunded, and no partial refunds for time already used.

The short version

  • First payment: a full refund if you ask within 7 days of your first paid charge for a workspace.
  • Duplicated or mistaken charges: the extra amount is always refunded, and your plan continues.
  • After that: no partial refunds for periods already used. Cancel any time and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Full refund within 7 days

If Handlark is not right for you, write to us within 7 days of the first paid charge for a workspace and we will refund that charge in full. This applies to monthly and yearly plans. It applies once per workspace and owner, to the first payment only, not to renewals. After the refund the plan ends and the workspace becomes read only.

Duplicated or mistaken charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, charged after you cancelled, or charged a wrong amount because of an error on our side, we refund the extra amount in full, whenever you notice it. Your plan carries on as normal: only a full refund of the payment for your current period ends the plan.

Periods already used

Apart from the cases above, we do not give partial or prorated refunds for the unused part of a month or year, or for downgrades. When you cancel, the plan stays active until the end of the current period and is not renewed.

How to cancel

Open the app, go to Settings, Plan and billing and cancel. It takes effect at the end of the period you have already paid for. You can also write to us and we will do it for you.

How to ask for a refund

Write to hello@example.com from the email address you sign in with. Tell us the workspace name, the date and amount of the charge and whether you paid in naira or US dollars.

  • Naira payments are refunded through Paystack to the card or account you paid with.
  • US dollar payments are refunded through Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record for those payments.
  • We confirm within 3 business days. The money usually reaches you within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Before a chargeback

Please write to us before disputing a charge with your bank. We can usually sort things out faster, and a chargeback may pause the workspace while it is reviewed.

Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any rights you have under consumer protection law in your country, including the Federal Competition and Consumer Protection Act in Nigeria.
