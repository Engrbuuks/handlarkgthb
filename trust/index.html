Kind by design

Handlark helps teams trust their hours without watching anyone: no keystroke logging, no webcam, no app or website tracking and no ranking of people. Screenshots happen only in Proof mode, for one client, with each person's recorded consent, and the person sees each one first. Everyone sees what is measured about them.

Certifications

Handlark has no SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate yet: those audits cost more than a young service can pay. Our providers hold their own (Supabase, Cloudflare, Google and GitHub, among others). We will publish ours when we have it. Meanwhile we answer security questionnaires: write to hello@example.com.
