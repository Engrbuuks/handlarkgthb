Data retention

How long each kind of record is kept, and when it is deleted. Every line is checked against the code that does it.

Last updated 17 October 2026

On this page
  1. The table
  2. What the owner controls

Nothing in a workspace is deleted while you use it, except what this table says. The scheduled job runs every 10 minutes and does the deleting. Your workspace's own data stays yours to export at any time.

What the owner controls

The owner can export everything at any time, delete tasks (to the bin), choose how long Proof mode screenshots are kept, delete people's time, and delete the whole workspace. Members can export what they can see and delete their own account.

Backups and deletion

Backups exist only to recover the service after a fault. When a workspace is purged, its data stays in database backups until they age out, within 13 months. If we ever restore a backup, we delete again anything that had already been deleted.
