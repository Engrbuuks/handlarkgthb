Sub processors

Every service that helps run Handlark: what it does, what it handles, where, and when it is used.

Last updated 17 October 2026

On this page
  1. Always used
  2. Used when a feature is on
  3. Services you connect yourself
  4. Not used

These are the services Handlark uses to provide the service. Some are used only when you switch a feature on. We announce changes here and to workspace owners at least 30 days before a new one starts.

Services you connect yourself

These are your own accounts and choices, not providers we pick, and they are not our sub processors: Slack, Microsoft Teams and Discord chat alerts, Zapier, Make, your own webhook addresses, your own Paystack account for client invoices and staff pay, and your own WhatsApp Business number. Handlark sends them only what you choose, with the same privacy rules as the app.

Not used

No analytics, advertising or tracking services, and no session recording. The uptime monitor that checks Handlark is up only loads public pages and holds no personal data.
