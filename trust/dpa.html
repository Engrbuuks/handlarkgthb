How to accept it: the workspace owner opens Settings, Data processing agreement, reads this agreement, types their name and accepts it for the business. Handlark records the name, the time and the version, shows the record in Settings and writes it to the history. Print this page to keep a copy.

1. Parties and scope

This agreement is between the business that owns a Handlark workspace (the Customer, the controller) and Handlark (Handlark, the processor). It applies to personal data that Handlark processes for the Customer when it provides the service under the terms. Where the terms and this agreement differ about personal data, this agreement wins.

2. Roles

The Customer decides why and how workspace content is processed and is the controller of it. Handlark processes it only to provide the service, on the Customer's documented instructions, which are the terms, this agreement and the settings the Customer chooses in the app. If Handlark believes an instruction breaks the law, it will say so. Handlark is the controller of account and billing data it needs to run its own business, as the privacy policy explains.

3. Details of the processing

Subject matterProviding Handlark: tasks, time, attendance, leave, payroll, invoices, meetings, client pages, insights, integrations and the browser extension
DurationWhile the workspace exists, then as in clause 10 and the retention table
Nature and purposeStoring, organising, showing, sending and deleting data so the Customer's team and clients can work together
People concernedThe Customer's staff and contractors, its clients' contacts, and anyone the Customer adds
Kinds of dataNames, email addresses, phone numbers if added, time zones, work content, time and clock records, location at clock in if switched on, leave, pay rates and payout details, invoices, messages and files, Proof mode screenshots if used
Special categoriesNone are needed. Leave types may reveal sickness; Handlark keeps the type of leave inside the workspace and never sends it through the API, webhooks or chat alerts. The Customer should not put other special categories into tasks without a lawful reason

4. Confidentiality

Anyone at Handlark who can reach personal data is bound to keep it confidential and reaches it only when needed to run, support or secure the service.

5. Security

Handlark keeps the measures in the annex and on the security page, and keeps them at least as strong for the life of this agreement.

6. Sub processors

The Customer authorises the sub processors on the sub processors page. Handlark binds each one to data protection terms at least as protective as these and stays responsible for them. Handlark announces a new or replacement sub processor on that page and to workspace owners by email or in the app at least 30 days before it starts; the Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds, and if no solution is found may end the service and get a refund of fees paid for the time after the change. Services the Customer connects itself (Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Zapier, Make, its own webhooks or its own Paystack) are the Customer's choice and not Handlark's sub processors.

7. Help with people's rights

The app lets the Customer find, export, correct and delete data. Where that is not enough, Handlark helps the Customer answer requests from the people concerned, and passes on any request it receives about the Customer's workspace.

8. Personal data breaches

Handlark tells the workspace owner without undue delay, and within 48 hours of becoming aware of a breach affecting the Customer's personal data, with what is known, the likely effects and the steps taken, and keeps them informed. This lets the Customer meet its own deadlines, such as 72 hours under the GDPR and the NDPA.

9. Assessments and authorities

Handlark gives reasonable help with data protection impact assessments and talks with authorities where the law requires, using the information in this trust centre first.

10. Deletion and return

The Customer can export everything at any time, even after a trial ends. When the owner deletes the workspace it can be restored for 30 days, then its records and files are purged from the live service. Encrypted backups age out within 13 months (deleted files within 35 days), and data restored from a backup is deleted again. See the retention table.

11. Information and audits

Handlark makes available the information needed to show it meets this agreement, through this trust centre and answers to reasonable questionnaires. Where the law requires more, the Customer may audit, at its own cost, with 30 days' notice, during working hours, at most once a year unless after a breach, under confidentiality, and without access to other customers' data.

12. Transfers

Data is stored in the United Kingdom (Supabase, London region) and some sub processors work in the United States, the European Union and elsewhere. Where personal data leaves Nigeria, the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, Handlark relies on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses and the other safeguards the NDPA and the GDPR allow, and the parties agree to those clauses where they are needed.

13. Liability, term and law

Liability under this agreement follows the limits in the terms. This agreement lasts as long as Handlark processes personal data for the Customer. It is governed by the law named in the terms. A newer version applies once the owner accepts it in the app; until then this version keeps applying.

Annex: security measures

  • Sign in by Google Firebase Authentication; Handlark never sees passwords. Every request's login is checked against Google's public keys.
  • Optional two step sign in, which the owner can require for everyone; limits on wrong codes.
  • Row level security on every database table; the public database roles have no access; every read and write goes through server functions that check membership, role and privacy rules.
  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS everywhere, with strict transport security) and at rest; keys and secrets the Customer gives Handlark are encrypted again with Handlark's own key.
  • Private file storage with links that last one hour, given only to people who can see the task; uploads checked for their real type.
  • A history of every change, with what it was before and after; sign out everywhere.
  • Nightly encrypted backups, checked by restoring each one, kept off site with a key held offline.
  • Strict security headers on every page; no tracking or advertising scripts.
