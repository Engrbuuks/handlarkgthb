Setup keys

These are made here in your browser and are not sent anywhere. Copy each one into Supabase: Edge Functions, Secrets. Then close this page. Making new ones later turns off phone alerts on every device until people switch them on again, and signs everyone out of two step sign in once.

About Copy: pressing Copy puts the secret on your clipboard. Clipboard history (Windows key and V, phone keyboards) or clipboard sync between your devices may keep it. Paste it straight into Supabase, then copy something harmless, such as a single word, so the secret is no longer the last thing copied. If your clipboard history is on, delete the entry there too.

This page is only needed during setup. Once the keys are saved in Supabase you may delete app/keys.html and app/keys.js from the live site and upload the folder again; the app does not use them.
