Clients, rates and client approval of hours

Client settings, rates and rounding, retainers, client page links and asking clients to approve hours before billing.

On this page
  1. Client settings
  2. The client page
  3. Client approval of hours

Client settings

Open a client under Settings, Clients: hourly rate and currency, a rate per person, rounding (none, 6, 15 or 30 minutes), billable by default, a monthly retainer in hours or money with alerts at 80% and 100%, the client email and the language of this client's emails (English or French).

The client page

Create client link makes the private page; send it to the client. Turn off link stops it at once. Choose what it shows: a detailed timesheet, time budgets, a daily summary email and a weekly report on Fridays.

Client approval of hours

Switch on Client approves hours before billing. The client approves or queries each line on their page; you reply under Insights, Time, adjusting minutes if needed. Bill hours then takes only approved lines.
