Security and signing in

Two step sign in, sign in with Google or Microsoft, signing out everywhere, and keeping your account safe.

On this page
  1. Two step sign in
  2. Google and Microsoft
  3. Signing out everywhere
  4. More

Two step sign in

In your account, turn on two step sign in with an authenticator app and keep the recovery codes. Owners can require it for everyone under Settings, Security. After 5 wrong codes, codes pause for 15 minutes.

Google and Microsoft

Continue with Google and Continue with Microsoft appear when the business that runs your Handlark switched them on. An existing account is linked only when the provider confirmed the same email, after you type your password once.

Signing out everywhere

Sign out everywhere in your account signs out every phone and computer at once, this one too. On shared computers, leave Trust this device off.
