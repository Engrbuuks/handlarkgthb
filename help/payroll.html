Payroll, payouts and payslips

Monthly payroll with overtime and leave, payout files for Wise, PayPal, Payoneer, Deel and Gusto, and paying staff through your own Paystack.

On this page

  2. Payout details

Payroll

Insights, Payroll shows monthly hours, overtime, late starts, leave, public holidays and gross pay before tax, with an export. Pay rates are set under Settings, People by the owner or a manager with Rates and pay.

Payout details

Each person adds how they are paid in their account under Payout details. Account numbers are stored encrypted and only the last 4 digits ever show. You get an email whenever your details change.

Paying people

Approve the month with Approve payroll, then make Payout files for Wise, PayPal, Payoneer, Deel or Gusto, or choose Pay people to send pay from your own Paystack balance with a fresh code from your authenticator app. Each person gets a payslip by email once paid.

Who approves and pays whom

The server works out every amount from the time clock, leave, holidays, rates and approved expenses; if what the page showed differs, approving is refused and you reload. A manager with Rates and pay approves and pays only the people they look after, never themselves: the owner approves and pays a manager. Approving again changes only the lines of the people you look after, so lines the owner approved stay.

Payout details someone other than the person changes wait for that person, or the owner, to confirm them (the person gets an email and a note in their account, Payout details); until then nobody is paid to them and they stay out of payout files. A manager never pays, or puts in a file, details they changed themselves. A manager's changes to pay are sent to the owner, and a rise of more than 25% over what the owner last set, a new pay type, a new currency or overtime rules that pay more (a higher multiplier, or overtime starting sooner) wait for the owner to approve them under People. Cuts apply at once. A manager can reopen only the lines of the people they look after whose pay has not gone out; the owner reopens the whole month.

Payout details someone other than the person changed in the last 7 days, you included, need a tick that you checked them with the person on a call. Send again repeats a transfer to the same account it was made for; if the details changed since, choose Check status, and when Paystack has no such transfer it is cancelled so you can pay the new account in a new payment.
