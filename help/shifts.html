Planned shifts, attendance and hour limits

Plan shifts once or every week, swap or drop them, see who was on time, and set weekly hour limits and overtime.

Planning shifts

Insights, Schedule, Plan shifts: a person, One day, Every week or Every other week, the days, start and end in that person's own time zone, and optionally a client and a work site. End this plan stops it from today.

Swaps

Swap or drop gives one shift to a teammate or drops it, with a reason. Your lead, a manager or the owner approves it, and both people are told.

Late, missed and left early

After a grace period (10 minutes by default), a shift with no clock in is told to you first, with Add reason. Only if nothing is explained within 15 minutes does your team lead hear. Leave and public holidays never count as missed.

Hour limits and overtime

Under Settings, Attendance and hours, a weekly hour limit gives you a note at 90% and tells your lead past 100%. Overtime rules pay hours past a daily and weekly limit at a multiplier in payroll.
