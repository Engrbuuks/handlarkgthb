The still working check and the idle check

Why Handlark sometimes asks "Still working?", how to answer, and what happens to time when nobody answers.

On this page
  1. What it is
  2. If nobody answers
  3. The optional idle check

What it is

While a timer or the clock runs and nothing has happened in Handlark for a while (45 minutes by default), you get a phone alert or a banner: Still working on ...? Answer Yes, keep going, Pause, or I stopped at a time.

If nobody answers

After 10 more minutes the time stops at your last activity plus 10 minutes. The gap is kept as unconfirmed time that you can claim with a note; the owner approves it. Time is never silently thrown away, and it is only paused when the question really reached you.

The optional idle check

If the owner turns it on, the Handlark tab notes only the moment of your last key, click, tap or scroll in that tab, and asks sooner when the tab has been quiet. Choose I often work in other apps and a quiet tab never counts against you. The browser extension offers an idle hint too, only if you switch it on.
