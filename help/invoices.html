Invoices and getting paid

Bill tracked hours, send invoices, take payment online, recurring invoices, reminders, part payments and credit notes.

Billing hours

Bill hours (Insights, Invoices, a client, or the Time view) makes a draft invoice from approved, unbilled time and locks those entries so they are never billed twice. Delete or void the invoice to free the time again.

Getting paid online

Connect your own Paystack under Settings, Get paid online and paste the webhook address it shows into Paystack. Clients then pay from their page, and the invoice marks itself paid. The money goes to you, never through Handlark.

A payment page works for 24 hours. If the client pays more than is still due (say a credit note lowered the invoice after they opened the page), or pays from a page that has expired, the money is never lost: what is due is applied, the rest is kept as a credit on the client, and you get an email. Find it under Insights, Invoices, Payments to sort out: refund it in Paystack and mark it refunded, or apply it to one of their open invoices, which records it there as a payment. While such a payment waits, that client gets no reminders and no late fees. An invoice is paid when its recorded payments and credits cover it, so removing a payment opens it again, and removing a credit you applied puts that amount back in the client's credit.

Recurring invoices and reminders

Recurring makes invoices weekly or monthly from approved hours or a retainer. Under Settings, Reminders, late fees and currencies, turn on friendly reminders on the days you choose and an optional late fee, off by default.

Part payments, credit notes and reports

Record payment takes part payments; Credit note lowers what is owed. Ageing and Receivables show what clients owe and how late, per currency.
