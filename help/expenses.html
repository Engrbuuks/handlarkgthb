Expenses

Claim expenses with receipts, approve them, repay them in payroll and bill them to clients.

On this page
  1. Claiming
  2. Approving and repaying

Claiming

Under Insights, Expenses, choose Add expense, with the amount, client and a photo or PDF of the receipt (up to 10 MB).

Approving and repaying

Your lead, a manager or the owner approves it. Approved expenses are repaid in payroll, and billable ones can go on the client's invoice.
