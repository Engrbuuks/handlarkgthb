Leave and public holidays

Ask for leave, see your balances, set leave types and allowances, and holidays for 12 countries.

Asking for leave

In your account choose Ask for leave (or Insights, Leave and holidays). Pick the type, the dates and, for one day, a half day. The request sheet shows what is left. Your lead, a manager or the owner approves it, and it comes off your balance.

Leave types and balances

Owners set types under Settings, Leave types: days a year, Earned each month or All at the start of the year, a carry over cap, paid or unpaid, and whether it Can go below zero. Balances count working days only.

Public holidays

Holidays for Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa, Egypt, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, the Philippines, India, Pakistan and Bangladesh are worked out for any year, by each person's country. Islamic holidays are marked as possibly moving. Add your own days under Settings.
