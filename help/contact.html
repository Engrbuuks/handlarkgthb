Ways to reach us

Email us from Help and support in the app or the button below. When the team offers them, WhatsApp and live chat are there too; live chat opens in a new tab.

What to include

What you did, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if you can. Report a problem fills in your app version and device for you.
