Getting started as an owner

Create your workspace, add clients and people, and get the first tasks moving in about ten minutes.

On this page
  1. Create your workspace
  2. Add your clients
  3. Invite your people
  4. Hand over the first tasks
  5. What to set up next

The owner is the person who made the workspace. You can do everything: people, clients, money, settings and the plan. Home shows a Getting started checklist until you finish it or hide it.

Create your workspace

  1. Open the app and choose Create an account. Confirm your email from the message Handlark sends.
  2. Name your workspace (usually your business), answer What kind of team are you? and choose your time zone for deadlines. See Your kind of team.
  3. Your free trial starts at once: 14 days, or 44 days if a customer invited you, with up to 15 people and no card.

The workspace starts with sample clients and templates so the screens are not empty. Rename them or delete them when you are ready.

Add your clients

Go to Settings, Clients and choose New client. A client has a type: Client for outside customers (time is billable by default) or Internal for your own work (not billable by default). Open a client to set its hourly rate, currency, client email and client page link.

Invite your people

Go to Settings, People and choose New person. Type their name and email and save. If email is set up they get an invitation; either way you can copy their invite link and send it yourself, for example on WhatsApp. Invite links work for 14 days.

Give each person a role: Member, Team lead or Manager. See Roles and teams.

Hand over the first tasks

Use the plus button (New task) and choose who it is for, the client, a due date and the priority. The person sees it on their Home straight away. Tick Needs my sign off if you want to check the work before it counts as done.

What to set up next

  • Phone alerts for yourself: Settings, Alerts, calendar and sign in, then Turn on for this device.
  • Daily summary email: Settings, Emails and deadlines.
  • Two step sign in, and require it for everyone under Settings, Security.
  • Get paid online with your own Paystack account, so clients pay invoices by card, transfer or USSD.
  • Accept the data processing agreement under Settings, Data processing agreement if your clients or your own policy need one.
