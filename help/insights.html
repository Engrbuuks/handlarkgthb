Overview

Owners, managers and team leads see hours, billable hours, utilization, on time against planned shifts, focus and meetings, unusual time and free hours ahead, with Export CSV and Print.

My patterns

Everyone sees their own focus and meeting time, utilization, capacity, unusual time and wellbeing signals first, privately, with a kind suggestion.

What is never done

No app or website tracking, no keyboard or mouse scores and no ranking: people are always listed by name. Leads see focus and meetings only as one total for everyone they look after, when that is three or more people, rounded to the hour, and never for a single team they pick: two teams side by side could give away one person's time. Each person counts only for the time they were in that lead's teams, so moving someone in or out never reveals their earlier time. Wellbeing shows the same way, only as counts, unless a person chooses to share.
