Your kind of team

Service firm, company, remote team or NGO: what the choice changes, the words for a client, and turning billing on or off.

On this page
  1. The four kinds
  2. We bill clients
  3. Changing it later

When you create a workspace, Handlark asks What kind of team are you? The answer changes the words and the menus, never your data.

The four kinds

  • Service firm or agency: you serve clients, such as accounting, law, consulting or a creative agency. Work is grouped by client, with client pages, rates and invoices.
  • Remote or outsourcing team: you serve clients from elsewhere, such as a virtual assistant agency. The same as a service firm.
  • Company or growing team: your own work. A client is called a Department, and the sample work starts with Operations, Sales and Admin.
  • NGO or field team: projects across locations. A client is called a Project, and work sites and attendance come first in Settings and Insights.

Workspaces made before version 1.16.0 are service firms, so nothing changed for them.

We bill clients

For a company or an NGO, Bill hours, invoices, rates, retainers and the billing parts of the client page stay out of the menus. If you do bill someone, turn on We bill clients under Settings, Workspace, Kind of team. Nothing is deleted or switched off either way: an invoice made earlier is still there.

Changing it later

The owner, or a manager allowed workspace settings, changes the kind of team under Settings, Workspace, Kind of team and chooses Save kind of team. The words change at once for everyone, in English and French. Names you typed, such as a department called Client Services, never change. The history of changes records who changed it.
