Phone alerts, WhatsApp and emails

Turn on phone alerts, WhatsApp alerts and replies, the calendar feed, and choose which emails you get.

On this page
  1. Phone alerts
  2. WhatsApp
  3. Emails
  4. Calendar feed

Phone alerts

In your account, under Phone alerts, choose Turn on for this device on each phone or computer. On an iPhone, first add Handlark to the Home Screen (Share, Add to Home Screen) and open it from there.

WhatsApp

When the owner turns WhatsApp on, add your number in your account and type the six digit code it sends. Reply TASKS, IN, OUT, DONE 2, ADD and more; STOP turns alerts off.

Emails

Switch reminders, the daily summary, weekly reports and meeting emails on or off under Emails in your account. Invitations and security emails always arrive. Every list email has a one click unsubscribe link.

Calendar feed

Your account has a private calendar link for Google, Apple or Outlook calendar: due tasks, planned starts and approved leave.
