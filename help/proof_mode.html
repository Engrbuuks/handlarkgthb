Proof mode

Optional screenshots for one client, only with each person's consent, seen by the person first and deletable.

On this page
  1. What it is
  2. Your consent
  3. You see them first
  4. Phones and the extension

What it is

Some clients ask for proof of hours. Proof mode is off unless the owner turns it on for that client. It takes 1 to 3 screenshots in each 10 minutes, blurred by default, only in your own browser while you share your screen and time runs for that client.

Your consent

The first time you add time for that client you see exactly what is captured, how often, who sees it and for how long, and you tick I have read this and I agree. The owner can never agree for you. Withdraw in your account stops it: nothing more is captured, and your screenshots for that client are deleted, except ones on time already approved or invoiced, which stay as the evidence for that time until they are deleted as usual.

You see them first

Every screenshot waits in your Proof tray for 10 minutes, where you can delete it. A deleted one leaves only a marker. Pause proof for 10 minutes is there for private moments.

Phones and the extension

Phones cannot share a screen, and the browser extension never captures one, so time for a Proof client starts in the web app on a computer.
