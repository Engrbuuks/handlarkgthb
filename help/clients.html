For clients: your client page

Follow progress, comment, approve finished work and hours, pay invoices and ask for work or meetings, with no account.

On this page
  1. What you see
  2. Add your name
  3. Approve work and hours
  4. Pay an invoice
  5. Ask for work or a meeting

Your client page is a private link the team sends you. Keep it to yourself: anyone with the link sees your page. You never need an account or a password.

What you see

Work in progress and finished work for you, comments, meetings you are invited to, invoices and, if the team chose, your hours this month and in all, a monthly retainer and a detailed timesheet. You never see private notes kept inside the team.

The page shows in your browser's language: English or French.

Add your name

Type your name at the top of the page so the team knows who commented or approved. It is kept on your device for next time.

Approve work and hours

For finished work, choose Approve, or comment if something needs changing. If the team asks you to approve hours, each line shows the date, first name, task and hours: choose Approve, or Query and say what looks wrong. The team replies on the page. Only approved hours are billed.

Pay an invoice

Open the invoice and choose Pay now to pay by card, bank transfer or USSD through Paystack, in naira, cedis, rand, Kenyan shillings or US dollars. You can pay part of it. The invoice updates straight away. If you paid another way, choose I have paid by transfer.

Ask for work or a meeting

Request something puts new work straight onto the team's list. Ask for a meeting lets you suggest up to three times; the team picks one and you get the link. For meetings you are invited to, say whether you can make it and join from the page.
