Plans and billing

Plans, the free trial, paying in naira or dollars, changing or cancelling, refunds and referral free months.

Plans

Every plan has every feature, priced per workspace, not per person, monthly or yearly. A year costs 10 months, so you get 2 months free. The starting prices are:

  • Starter (up to 5 people): $15 or ₦12,000 a month, or $150 or ₦120,000 a year
  • Team (up to 15 people): $39 or ₦30,000 a month, or $390 or ₦300,000 a year
  • Agency (up to 50 people): $99 or ₦75,000 a month, or $990 or ₦750,000 a year

The free trial allows up to 15 people. The prices today, and any founding offer, are on the sales page and under Settings, Plan and billing. A price change only ever applies to new subscribers: once you subscribe, you keep the price you signed up at.

The founding offer

While a founding offer runs, the sales page and Settings, Plan and billing show the percent off, the end date and the spots left. Subscribe while it is on and the discount stays on your workspace for life, also when you change plan later. A spot is held for 2 hours while you are at the payment page.

Paying

The owner pays under Settings, Plan and billing: in naira with Paystack (card, transfer or USSD) or in US dollars with Lemon Squeezy. Cancel plan, Resume plan, Change plan and Update card are all there.

When a trial or plan ends

The workspace becomes read only: nothing is deleted, and everyone can still export what they can see. Choose a plan to carry on.

Refunds and referrals

A full refund within 7 days of the first paid charge (see Refunds). Share your link under Settings, Refer and earn: the business you invite gets 44 free days, and you get a free month when they pay.
