Plans and billing

Plans, the free trial, paying in naira or dollars, changing or cancelling, refunds and referral free months.

On this page

  2. Paying
  3. When a trial or plan ends
  4. Refunds and referrals

Plans

Every plan has every feature. Starter is for up to 5 people, Team up to 15 and Agency up to 50, priced per workspace, not per person, monthly or yearly (two months free). Prices are on the sales page.

Paying

The owner pays under Settings, Plan and billing: in naira with Paystack (card, transfer or USSD) or in US dollars with Lemon Squeezy. Cancel plan, Resume plan, Change plan and Update card are all there.

When a trial or plan ends

The workspace becomes read only: nothing is deleted, and everyone can still export what they can see. Choose a plan to carry on.

Refunds and referrals

A full refund within 7 days of the first paid charge (see Refunds). Share your link under Settings, Refer and earn: the business you invite gets 44 free days, and you get a free month when they pay.
