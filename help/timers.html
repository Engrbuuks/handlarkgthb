Timers, focus sessions and timesheets

Time a task, add time by hand, send your week for approval, and how time budgets and notes work.

The task timer

Open a task and choose Start timer. One timer runs at a time: starting another stops the first. If you are clocked in for a client, the clock moves to the task, so nothing counts twice. A timer that runs past 10 hours, or 2 hours past the end of your working day, stops by itself and is flagged. You can also time from the browser extension.

Notes and budgets

Time longer than 30 minutes asks for a line on what you did when it stops. A task can have a time budget set by whoever made it; at 80% you get a gentle nudge, and past 100% adding time asks for a short reason. Your own estimate stays private and is never used to judge you.

Focus sessions

A focus session is a timed stretch on one task (5 to 50 minutes) with a calm screen and a place to park stray thoughts, which become tasks when it ends.

Timesheets

Under Insights, Time, My week, choose Send for approval. Your team lead, a manager or the owner approves it or sends it back with a note. Approved time can be billed.
