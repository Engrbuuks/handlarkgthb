Roles and teams

Owner, Manager, Team lead and Member: who sees and does what, and how teams send requests to the right person.

On this page
  1. The four roles
  2. Teams
  3. Hidden people

The four roles

RoleDoes
OwnerEverything, including the plan, the history, export everything, two step sign in for everyone, the Paystack key and Proof mode
ManagerPeople, tasks, approvals, shifts and reports for everyone or chosen teams; rates, billing and settings only when granted
Team leadApprovals, shifts, time and attendance for their team's members; no money and no people settings
MemberTheir own work

Clients are never users: they keep their private client page link.

Teams

Under Settings, Teams, choose New team, give it a name, one or more team leads and the members. A person can be in several teams. Requests go to the person's team leads first, then a manager who looks after them, then the owner.

Hidden people

The owner can hide a person from everyone else. Their work never reaches members, the client page shows them as Team member, and only the owner looks after them.
