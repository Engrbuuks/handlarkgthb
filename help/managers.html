Getting started as a manager

What a manager can do, which people you look after, and the three extra rights an owner can grant.

On this page
  1. What you can do
  2. Rights the owner can add
  3. What stays with the owner

A Manager runs people, tasks, approvals, planned shifts and reports for the people they look after: everyone, or the teams the owner chose.

What you can do

  • Add people (as members) and change the people you look after, including their hours, weekly hour limit, country for public holidays and leave allowance.
  • Approve timesheets, leave, expense claims, time claims and shift swaps for the people you look after.
  • Plan shifts, see attendance, time, review flags and the Insights for your people.
  • Edit or remove someone's time and clock records.

Rights the owner can add

Under Settings, People, the owner can tick three switches for you, each on its own: Rates and pay (pay and bill rates, payroll, payouts), Invoices and client billing (invoices, client pages, client emails and rates) and Workspace settings (work sites, holidays, leave types, attendance, hour limits, webhooks and chat alerts).

What stays with the owner

The plan and its payments, the history of changes, export everything, two step sign in for everyone, the Paystack key and Proof mode. Your own requests go to the owner, never to yourself.
