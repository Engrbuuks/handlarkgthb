Getting started as a member

Your Home, your tasks, the timer and the time clock: everything you need on your first day.

On this page
  1. Join your workspace
  2. Your Home
  3. Doing the work
  4. Clocking in
  5. Your account

Welcome. Handlark shows you your own work first. Nothing is hidden from you about what is measured about you, and nothing is recorded without you seeing it.

Join your workspace

Open the invite link your owner sent, create an account with any email and a password, and you land in the workspace. If you already have an account, sign in and the workspace appears.

Your Home

Home greets you, shows a line about your own day (a meeting soon, something waiting for you, or a short tip), the time clock, today's schedule and your clients. See Home and team time.

Doing the work

  • Open a task to read it, tick its steps, post a progress update or ask a question.
  • Start the timer on the task while you work, or start a focus session.
  • When you finish, choose Mark done and add one line on what you did, a link or a file.

Clocking in

Use Clock in on Home when your working day starts, Take a break for breaks, and Clock out with a short handoff note. See The time clock.

Your account

Tap your initials (on a computer, Settings) for your account: phone alerts, your language, your calendar link, two step sign in, leave, payout details, API tokens, the browser extension and export.
