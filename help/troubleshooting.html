Troubleshooting

Fixes for the usual problems: offline changes, alerts that do not arrive, sign in, uploads, a stopped timer and a read only workspace.

On this page

I was offline

Changes made offline are kept on the device and sent when you reconnect; a banner says how many are waiting. Keep the tab open until it says you are back online. Files can only be added while online.

Alerts do not arrive

  • Check alerts are on in your account on this device, and allowed for the site in your browser or phone settings.
  • On an iPhone, alerts work only from the Home Screen app.
  • Quiet hours hold alerts until your working day starts.

I cannot sign in

Use Forgot your password? on the sign in screen. If your workspace requires two step sign in, have your authenticator app ready, or use a recovery code. After many wrong codes, wait 15 minutes.

My timer stopped by itself

Timers stop past 10 hours or 2 hours past your working day, and time pauses when the still working check got no answer. Claim the time with a note under Insights, Time, My week.

The workspace is read only

The trial or plan has ended. The owner chooses a plan under Settings, Plan and billing. Nothing is deleted meanwhile.

Emails do not arrive

Look in spam and promotions. Check that your email is right under your account and that you did not unsubscribe from those emails. The owner can see whether email is set up under Settings.

Report a problem

Under Help and support, Report a problem, an email opens with your app version and device filled in.
