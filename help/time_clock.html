Clock in and out

On Home, Clock in starts your working day; Take a break and End break pause it; Clock out ends it with an optional handoff note that goes into the daily summary. A shift left open for 16 hours closes by itself and is flagged for a check.

Clocking in for a client

For a client clocks you in for a client and, if you like, a task. Your hours count for that client until you switch or clock out. Switch client moves the clock without clocking out. Breaks are never billed.

Correcting a shift

Tap a shift under Insights, Time clock to correct its times. A shift with time a client approved needs a reason, and the client is asked again.

Location and work sites

If the owner left it on, your phone may ask for your location when you clock in or out. Say no and you still clock in. Work sites (owners, Settings, Work sites) mark a shift Away from site when it is outside the site's radius. Nothing is ever blocked, and members never see anyone else's location.
