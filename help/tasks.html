Tasks

Making tasks

The plus button opens New task. Choose One task, Several (one per line, pasted straight from a message), a Template or From text (AI turns a message or a voice note into tasks, when it is switched on). Give it a client, who it is assigned to, a due date and time, a priority, steps and details.

Statuses and sign off

Tasks move through New, In progress, Waiting and Done. With Needs my sign off ticked, finished work goes back to the person who asked for it, who signs it off or sends it back with a note. Marking work done asks for one line on what you did, a link or a file.

Repeating tasks and templates

A task can repeat every day, every weekday, every week or every month; when it is done the next one is made. Templates (owners, under Settings, Templates) hold a list of steps; a step written as Reconcile bank accounts +2 [2h] falls two days after the start date with a time budget of two hours.

Private tasks and lists

If the owner allows it (Can keep private lists under People), you can keep private tasks and private lists that only you see. They never reach the owner, clients, exports of others, the AI or the API, and their time is never billed.

Importing

Tasks, Import takes an Excel workbook, a CSV file, cells pasted from a spreadsheet, or a Trello board export. Trello lists are matched to statuses, and checklists become steps.

Deleting and the archive

Deleted tasks wait in Tasks, Bin for 30 days and can be restored by the person who made them or the owner. Finished tasks move to Tasks, Archive after 90 days; search and reopen them there.
