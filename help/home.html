Home and team time

The line at the top of Home, tips, the team pill, team time, best overlap and quiet hours.

On this page
  1. The line at the top
  2. Team time
  3. Quiet hours

The line at the top

It rotates every 12 seconds: lines about your own day first (a meeting soon, approvals waiting, a question for you, overdue work), with short tips between them. Tap a line to go there. Pause stops it; with reduced motion on in your device it changes only now and then. Switch tips off in your account (Rotating tips on Home).

Team time

The Team pill shows how many people are working now. Tap it for everyone's local time, where their day is and their working hours in your time. Best overlap is the stretch today when most people are working; Set up a meeting in this window books it. Nobody's location is shown, only times.

Quiet hours

With Respect quiet hours on (the default), phone and WhatsApp alerts to someone outside their working hours wait until their day starts. Urgent alerts never wait: a meeting about to start, the still working check, payments and codes.
