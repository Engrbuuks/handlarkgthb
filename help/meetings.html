Meetings

Meetings with free video rooms, agendas, notes that become tasks, reminders, and meetings with clients.

On this page
  1. Making a meeting
  2. Notes and actions
  3. With clients

Making a meeting

Calendar, Meetings, New meeting: title, time, people, an agenda and the video room. Each meeting gets a free room on meet.jit.si, or paste your own Zoom, Google Meet or Teams link. Invitations go by email, phone alerts and WhatsApp when those are on.

Notes and actions

Write notes during the meeting; action items become tasks with an owner and a due date. Write summary with AI appears when AI is set up.

With clients

Clients see meetings they are invited to on their page, say whether they can come and join from there, and can ask for a meeting by suggesting up to three times.
