AI summaries and voice notes

Daily AI summaries, tasks from text and voice notes, and what never reaches the AI.

On this page
  1. What it does
  2. What never reaches the AI

What it does

When AI is set up, owners get Write summary for the team or one client, everyone gets From text to turn a message or a voice note into tasks, and meetings get a summary with action items. Each workspace has 60 AI requests a day.

What never reaches the AI

Private tasks, private lists, and the work, hours and leave of hidden people are left out of every request. Your data is never used to train AI. Voice notes are turned into text and never stored.
