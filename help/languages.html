Choosing your language

Handlark follows your browser's language at first. To choose, open your account (owners: Settings) and pick Language: follow my browser, English or Français. The app reloads in that language on every device you use, and your emails follow it.

What is translated

In version 1.15.0: the navigation, Home, the task sheet, the time clock, the client page, your account and help, and the main emails (invitations, new tasks, the still working check, leave, meetings, payslips, client summaries, weekly reports, invoices and reminders). Other screens are still in English while we translate them; anything not yet translated shows in English, never blank.

For clients

The client page follows the client's own browser. Set the language of a client's emails on the client under Settings, Clients.
