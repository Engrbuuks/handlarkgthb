Getting started as a team lead

Approve your team's time and leave, plan their shifts and keep an eye on attendance, without any money settings.

On this page
  1. Approvals
  2. Shifts and attendance
  3. Insights for your team

A Team lead looks after the members of the teams they lead. You approve, plan and see; money and people settings stay with others.

Approvals

Timesheets, leave, expense claims, time claims and shift swaps from your team come to you first. Find them under Insights (Time, Expenses, Schedule, Leave and holidays) and on the line at the top of Home when something waits.

Shifts and attendance

Plan shifts for your team under Insights, Schedule, Plan shifts. If a shift starts without a clock in, the person is told first and can give a reason; you hear only if nothing is explained in time.

Insights for your team

You see hours, billable hours, utilization, capacity and the unusual time report for your team. Focus and meeting time show only as one total for everyone you look after, when that is three or more people, and wellbeing only as counts. See Kind insights.
