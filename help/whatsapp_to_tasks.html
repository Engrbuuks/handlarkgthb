WhatsApp group chats into tasks

Forward a group message to Handlark, or upload a chat you exported, and keep the tasks you choose.

On this page
  1. Forward a message to Handlark
  2. Who a task can be for
  3. Export a chat from WhatsApp
  4. Upload a chat and pick the tasks

WhatsApp does not let any app read your groups, and Handlark never does. You choose what to share, in one of two ways: forward a message to the Handlark number, or upload a chat you exported.

Forward a message to Handlark

Turn on WhatsApp in your account first (see Phone alerts, WhatsApp and emails). Then, in any chat or group, press and hold a message, choose Forward and pick the Handlark number. You can also just type the task to Handlark in a sentence.

Handlark replies with what it understood, for example: Add this as a task? 'Send Brooks the October report' for you, due Friday 24 Oct, client Brooks Dental. Reply YES to add it, or NO.

  • YES (or OUI) adds it. NO (or NON) drops it. A suggestion waits 30 minutes; a new one replaces it.
  • A forwarded message is only ever a suggestion, never a command, so a forwarded DONE 1 from a group changes nothing.
  • If your number is in more than one workspace, Handlark first asks which one the task is for: reply 1, 2 and so on. Every reply names the workspace.
  • The task notes keep the original message, and its history says it came from WhatsApp.
  • When AI is on, it reads that one message to find the title, the date and an existing client. It never makes a new client. Without AI, the first line of the message is the title, for you, with no date.
  • Very short messages (ok, thanks, an emoji) and answers to Handlark's own messages are not turned into tasks. Pictures, files and other media cannot become tasks; voice notes can.

Who a task can be for

If the message names someone, the task is for them when your role allows it: the owner can give tasks to anyone, a manager or team lead to the people they look after. A member's tasks are for themselves. If you cannot give it to the person named, the task is yours and the reply says why.

Export a chat from WhatsApp

On Android: open the group, tap the three dots, then More, Export chat and Without media. Save the file or send it to yourself.

On iPhone: open the group, tap its name at the top, scroll down to Export Chat and choose Without Media. Save it to Files.

You get a .txt file, or a .zip with the chat inside. Either works.

Upload a chat and pick the tasks

  1. Choose New task, then From text, then From a WhatsApp chat, and choose the file. It is read on your device.
  2. Choose the period: the last day, 7 days (the default), 30 days, or since a date. If the dates could be either way round, choose day first or month first.
  3. What will be sent shows how many messages and senders go to the AI. Phone numbers and emails are taken out first, and a sender saved only as a number shows as Contact 1, Contact 2. At most about 40,000 characters are read; if the period holds more, the most recent messages are kept.
  4. Choose Find tasks. Each task shows who it is for (names from the chat are matched to your people), the date, the client and the message it came from. Change who it is for, edit the title, untick what you do not want, then Add.

Nothing from the chat is stored. Only the tasks you add are saved, and the history says how many came from a WhatsApp chat. Finding tasks needs AI and counts as one AI request for every part of the chat read, at most three.
