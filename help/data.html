Your data: export, history and deleting

Export everything as spreadsheets, the history of changes, leaving a workspace, deleting your account or a workspace.

On this page
  1. Export
  2. History
  3. Leaving and deleting

Export

Owners: Settings, Export everything downloads a ZIP of spreadsheets. Everyone else: Export what I can see in their account. Both work even after a trial ends.

History

The owner sees every change with what it was before and after, filtered by person, words and dates. Secrets never appear in it.

Leaving and deleting

Leave this workspace deletes your profile there; your shared work stays. Delete my account needs your password. The owner can delete a workspace and restore it for 30 days; after that it is purged. See the data retention table.
